About

This page plays a ROM you own, made playable in the browser with Game Printer, in the original's art or in a theme.

"Load your own ROM" accepts a Donkey Kong ROM file that you own. The page checks and reads it in your browser. It is never uploaded.

This page is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo. Donkey Kong is a trademark of Nintendo, named here only to say which file is accepted.

No analytics, no cookies, no tracking. Your settings, and a ROM you load with the pieces read from it, stay in this browser's storage on your device, so "Play my ROM version" can read it again on a later visit. "Forget my ROM data" removes the ROM and its pieces.
